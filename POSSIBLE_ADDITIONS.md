# Possible new additions

This is the entry point for mathematical extensions, not corrections to the
official Stacks text. To inspect only possible errors in inherited text, use
[Possible fixes](PROPOSED_CORRECTIONS.md) and its
[individually downloadable patches](possible-fixes/README.md).

The descriptions below identify what was drafted and where to read it.
"In the draft" does not mean accepted by the Stacks Project, independently
human-verified, or proof-assistant-checked. Existing Stacks coverage is not
counted as a new contribution. Repairs to our own additions are not upstream
errata.

For each accepted new addition, retain the original-work locator, a readable
statement and proof, explicit hypotheses and dependencies, comparison with
existing Stacks results, and the exact draft source. Keep conjectures and
conditional results labeled. A reader may reuse one addition without adopting
the rest of this project.
