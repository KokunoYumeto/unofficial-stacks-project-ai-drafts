## 1. Closure of compact objects

For an object P and a family (M_j), denote the canonical map by

\[
 \theta_{P,(M_j)}:\bigoplus_j\operatorname{Hom}(P,M_j)
       \longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}(P,\bigoplus_jM_j).
\]

Its restriction to the j-th summand composes with the actual coproduct
inclusion. Compactness is bijectivity of this map for every family.
Shifts preserve coproducts because they are equivalences; the shift
isomorphisms identify theta for P[r] and (M_j) with theta for P
and (M_j[-r]). Thus shifts preserve compactness. Isomorphisms do
also, by precomposition.

For a distinguished triangle X->Y->Z->X[1], apply Hom(-,M_j)
and take the direct sum of its exact sequences of abelian groups.
Compare with Hom(-,direct sum M_j). The five adjacent terms

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(Y[1],-)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Hom}(X[1],-)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Hom}(Z,-)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Hom}(Y,-)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Hom}(X,-)
\]

are exact with the triangle's given connecting signs. If X and Y
are compact, the four comparison maps surrounding the Z term
are isomorphisms. The five lemma makes the Z comparison an
isomorphism. Rotations handle every other pair of compact terms.
Finite sums preserve compactness, since Hom out of a finite
biproduct is a finite product and finite products of abelian
groups commute with direct sums.

If P is a retract of a compact Q, choose i:P->Q and r:Q->P
with ri=id_P. Precomposition with r and i makes the comparison
map for P a retract, in the category of homomorphisms, of that
for Q. Its inverse is the corresponding composite of the inverse
for Q with these inclusion and retraction maps; the equations
ri=id_P prove both inverse identities. Thus P is compact.

The previously proved original lemma-projectors-have-images-triangulated,
805–822, makes the ambient pre-triangulated category Karoubian
when it has countable coproducts: split monomorphisms have cokernels
by their split triangles, and the countable-coproduct idempotent
splitting criterion applies. Its actual summands are compact by
the preceding retract calculation. This proves every assertion
at 11146–11165, including the pre-triangulated version, without
using TR4 in the compactness comparison.

## 2. The cellular tower and the missing inverse shift — DERIVED-NEW-039

Let (E_i) be the source's compact family and assume their coproduct
is a weak generator. For a fixed X put

\[
 X_1=\bigoplus_{(i,m,\varphi:E_i[m]\to X)}E_i[m],
 \qquad p_1|_{(i,m,\varphi)}=\varphi.
\]

This is a set-indexed coproduct: I and Z are sets and each Hom
collection is a set. Given p_n:X_n->X let Y_n be the coproduct
indexed by maps phi:E_i[m]->X_n with p_n phi=0. Its component
map is v_n:Y_n->X_n. Complete it to the actual triangle

\[
 Y_n\xrightarrow{v_n}X_n\xrightarrow{u_n}X_{n+1}
          \xrightarrow{d_n}Y_n[1].
\]

The equality p_n v_n=0 follows on every coproduct component.
Exactness of Hom(-,X) supplies p_{n+1} with p_{n+1}u_n=p_n.
There is no uniqueness requirement on this choice.

Put S=direct sum_n X_n and
t i_n=i_n-i_{n+1}u_n, with i_n the coproduct inclusions.
Choose the telescope triangle

\[
 S\xrightarrow t S\xrightarrow q H\xrightarrow w S[1].
\]

The component maps p_n define p:S->X with pt=0, so exactness
gives f:H->X satisfying fq=p. Set q_n=q i_n.
Complete f to C --a--> H --f--> X --b--> C[1].
For any P=E_i[m], compactness and the previously proved telescope
comparison give

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(P,H)=
 \mathop{\rm colim}_n\operatorname{Hom}(P,X_n).
\]

If z:P->C, write az=q_n h for some h:P->X_n. Since faz=0
and fq_n=p_n, we have p_n h=0. The summand labelled h in Y_n
shows u_n h=0. Therefore az=q_{n+1}u_n h=0. The inverse-rotated
triangle is

\[
 X[-1]\xrightarrow{-b[-1]}C\xrightarrow a H\xrightarrow f X.
\]

Exactness supplies y:P->X[-1] with z=(-b[-1])y. Shift y to
y[1]:P[1]->X. Since P[1] is a shift of E_i, it labels a
summand of X_1. Write e:P[1]->X_1 for its inclusion. Then
p_1 e=y[1]. The shifted map

\[
 P\xrightarrow{e[-1]}X_1[-1]\xrightarrow{q_1[-1]}H[-1]
\]

composed with f[-1] is y, using the specified shift
identifications. Since (-b[-1])f[-1]=0, z=0.
Hence Hom(E_i[m],C)=0 for every i,m. The coproduct universal
property gives Hom(direct sum_i E_i,C[-m])=0 for every m,
so generation forces C to be zero and f to be an isomorphism.

The map just constructed lands in H[-1], not H. The wording
at 11209 omits [-1] from the displayed homotopy colimit.
The corrected object is (hocolim X_n)[-1]. This is a type
correction to the proof, not a change in the conclusion or a
choice of an unspecified map H->X[-1]. All choices of lifts
p_{n+1} and f satisfy the argument.

## 3. Exact finite-stage factorization and its level — DERIVED-UNDER-029

Here a stronger hypothesis accounting is useful. Let (E_i) be
any family of compact objects in a triangulated category with
coproducts. A weak-generation assumption is not needed in this
section. Suppose X_1 is a coproduct of shifts of this family and
there are triangles

\[
 Y_{n-1}\xrightarrow u X_{n-1}\xrightarrow v X_n
        \xrightarrow d Y_{n-1}[1]
\]

with each Y_{n-1} a coproduct of such shifts. For any compact C
and map h:C->X_n there exist a finite J subset I, an object
Q in <direct sum_{j in J} E_j>_n and actual maps

\[
 C\xrightarrow s Q\xrightarrow r X_n,\qquad rs=h.
\]

The subscript n is a proved extension-cost bound, not a claim
of minimality. It strengthens the source's unbounded membership
in <direct sum E_j>. Generation is used in Section 2 to obtain
the isomorphism from the telescope, but not for this finite-stage
factorization.

For n=1 compactness factors h through a finite subcoproduct Q.
Its objects are shifts of a finite family, so Q is in level one.
Suppose the statement holds for n-1. Compactness factors dh
through a finite subcoproduct of Y_{n-1}[1]. Write it as

\[
 C\xrightarrow\delta E'[1]\xrightarrow{j[1]}Y_{n-1}[1],
 \qquad j[1]\delta=dh,
\]

where E' is a finite direct sum of shifts of the E_i with
indices I'. Complete delta to a triangle
E' --a--> C' --b--> C --delta--> E'[1].
TR3 applied to the rotated triangles gives a map t:C'->X_{n-1}
such that (j,t,h) is a morphism to the preceding displayed
triangle: ta=uj, v t=h b, and j[1]delta=d h.
In particular C' is compact by
Section 1.

Induction factors t as C' --alpha--> E'' --beta--> X_{n-1}
with E'' in level n-1 for a finite family I''. Complete
alpha a to

\[
 E'\xrightarrow{\alpha a}E''\xrightarrow l E
                     \xrightarrow e E'[1].
\]

TR3 gives p:C->E and q:E->X_n making (id,alpha,p) and
(j,beta,q) morphisms of triangles. Thus e p=delta and
d q=j[1]e. It follows exactly that

\[
 d(h-qp)=dh-j[1]ep=dh-j[1]\delta=0.
\]

The common target in this comparison is Y_{n-1}[1]. The source
at 11255 prints Y_{n-1}, missing [1]. This is
DERIVED-NEW-040. Exactness of Hom(C,-) now provides
z:C->X_{n-1} with vz=h-qp. Inductively factor
z as C --gamma--> E''' --eta--> X_{n-1}, using a finite
family I''' and level n-1. The complete factorization is

\[
 C\xrightarrow{\binom p\gamma}E\oplus E'''
       \xrightarrow{(q,\ v\eta)}X_n,
 \qquad (q,\ v\eta)\binom p\gamma=qp+v\eta\gamma=h.
\]

It preserves the original h, not merely its image under d.
The objects E'' and E''' are different induction outputs.
This verifies the already applied MC-STK-ERR-0895 replacement
of E'' by E''' at 11258 and MC-STK-ERR-0896 replacement of
I' by I''' at 11259. In particular I''' is a finite index set,
not an “injective object” as the P02-E0089 explanation calls it.

To prove the cost bound, put J=I' union I'' union I''' and
G_J=direct sum_{j in J}E_j. Enlarging a finite family preserves
membership in each level: the smaller direct sum is a specified
summand of G_J, so replace its leaf witnesses by those summands.
We have E' in level one, E'',E''' in level n-1. Rotation of
the E triangle gives E''->E->E'[1], hence E is in level n
by the level-addition formula proved in the preceding checkpoint.
Levels are increasing and closed under finite sums, so
E+E''' is in level n as required. If J is empty, all these
objects are zero and the empty finite sum is the generator 0.
Every membership also has a finite shift window by the finite
witness argument of PROOFS_10691_11108.md, Section 5.

## 4. Compact generators and the family conclusion

If compact E classically generates D_c and D is compactly
generated by a family (F_i), then Hom(E,K[r])=0 for all r
implies Hom(F_i,K[r])=0 for all i,r. Indeed F_i belongs to
<E>, and the right-orthogonal calculation already proved at
original 11033–11050 applies to every shift of K. Generation
by direct sum F_i forces K=0. This proves (1) implies (2).

Conversely a compact weak generator E gives the source's
one-element compact generating set. For compact X use the tower
of Section 2 and its isomorphism f:H->X. Compactness factors
f^{-1}:X->H through a stage: f^{-1}=q_n z. Put r=f q_n.
Then rz=id_X. Apply Section 3 to z to factor it through a
finite object Q in <E>_n. Thus X is an actual retract of Q
and hence is in <E>_n, which is closed under summands.
The complement exists in the Karoubian ambient category; its
compactness also follows from Section 1. The reverse inclusion
<E> subset D_c follows from compact closure. Therefore
D_c=<E>, proving the proposition.

Exactly the same argument for a family yields

\[
 D_c=\bigcup_{\substack{J\subset I\ {\rm finite}\\ n\geq1}}
          \left\langle\bigoplus_{j\in J}E_j\right\rangle_n .
\]

The actual retract uses f,q_n,z and the Section 3 factorization.
This is recorded with UNDER-029 as its family consequence,
not as an independent novelty claim.
MC-STK-ERR-0897 correctly changes “the lemma is proven” at
11309 to “the proposition is proven”. Its earlier registry
description gives an inaccurate label; the actual source label
is proposition-generator-versus-classical-generator.

## 5. Brown's representing object and its exact transformation

Now let H:D^op->Ab be cohomological and take arbitrary coproducts
to products, with D compactly generated. Enlarge the set of
compact generators by all integer shifts, retaining the actual
objects and their shift isomorphisms; use that indexed set below.
For each pair (i,a) with a in H(E_i), take a copy of E_i:

\[
 X_1=\bigoplus_{(i,a)}E_i,\qquad
 a_1\in H(X_1)\cong\prod_{(i,a)}H(E_i),\quad
 a_1=(a)_{(i,a)}.
\]

The product comparison is the one induced by all inclusions.
The associated Yoneda transformation is explicitly
theta_{1,W}(f)=H(f)(a_1) for f:W->X_1.

Suppose (X_n,a_n) has been chosen. For all pairs (i,k) with
k:E_i->X_n and H(k)a_n=0, let K_{n+1} be their coproduct
and g_n:K_{n+1}->X_n their component map. The product
comparison gives H(g_n)a_n=0 because each coordinate is zero.
Complete g_n to

\[
 K_{n+1}\xrightarrow{g_n}X_n\xrightarrow{u_n}X_{n+1}
                       \longrightarrow K_{n+1}[1].
\]

Cohomological exactness gives a_{n+1} with H(u_n)a_{n+1}=a_n.
This justifies the source's use of the kernel functor without
assuming that this kernel is cohomological. Its needed product
property follows because kernels commute with products, or
directly from the coordinate calculation just given.

Form S=direct sum X_n and the telescope triangle
S --t--> S --q--> X --w--> S[1], with
t i_n=i_n-i_{n+1}u_n. Under H(S)=product H(X_n), H(t)
is the homomorphism

\[
 (b_n)_n\longmapsto
           (b_n-H(u_n)b_{n+1})_n .
\]

The tuple (a_n) is in its kernel. Exactness supplies a in H(X)
with H(q)a=(a_n). Put theta_W(f)=H(f)a for f:W->X.
Naturality follows from H(fg)=H(g)H(f), and
theta_W(q_n f)=H(f)a_n. This is the exact transformation
asserted at 11385–11387. The requested copyedit inserts the
finite verb in “Hence there is a natural transformation”.

For each shifted generator E_i, compactness identifies
Hom(E_i,X) with colim Hom(E_i,X_n). The transformation is
surjective there because the pair (i,b) for every b in H(E_i)
occurs in X_1. If the image of a class represented by
f:E_i->X_n is zero, H(f)a_n=0. It is a summand label of
K_{n+1}, so u_n f=0. Its colimit class is zero. Hence theta
is also injective on every shifted generator.

For completeness, let D' consist of Y for which theta_{Y[r]}
is an isomorphism for every integer r. Isomorphism invariance
and shift closure are immediate from this definition. Two
objects of a distinguished triangle in D' force the third
into D' by the five adjacent terms of the contravariant exact
sequences and the five lemma. A summand is handled by the
same explicit retraction of comparison maps as in Section 1.
Both Hom(-,X) and H send coproducts to products; a product
of the isomorphisms theta_{Y_j[r]} is an isomorphism.
Thus D' is closed under coproducts. In particular the
telescope triangle of any sequence of its objects has all
three terms in D'.

Every generator is in D', hence all X_1,Y_n and X_n in the
cellular presentation of every object are in D', by induction.
Section 2 gives every such object as that telescope. Hence
D'=D and theta is an isomorphism on every object, as required.
No assertion that the original generators classically generate
all of D is made.

Different choices of representing data give canonically
isomorphic representing pairs: if theta:Hom(-,X)->H and
theta':Hom(-,X')->H are the isomorphisms, Yoneda turns
(theta')^{-1}theta into a unique u:X->X'. Its inverse
comes from theta^{-1}theta', so both composites are identities.
This accounts for the nonunique choices made in the construction.

## 6. The exact right adjoint

For an exact coproduct-preserving F:D->D', fix Y in D' and
apply Section 5 to
H_Y(W)=Hom_{D'}(F(W),Y). Exactness of F and the contravariant
representable Hom sequence make this cohomological. The
coproduct universal property and preservation by F make
H_Y(direct sum W_j)=product H_Y(W_j).

Choose a representing object G(Y) and the natural bijections

\[
 \phi_{W,Y}:\operatorname{Hom}_D(W,G(Y))
        \xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Hom}_{D'}(F(W),Y).
\]

For a:Y->Y', postcomposition by a and these bijections give
a natural transformation Hom(-,G(Y))->Hom(-,G(Y')).
Yoneda defines G(a). The identities and composites are
preserved because their natural transformations are, proving
that G is a functor and that phi is natural in both variables.
This proves the actual adjunction, rather than objectwise
representability alone.

The shift comparison and preservation of triangles are proved
in PROOFS_1844_2401.md, Section 3, with the original
2290–2370 definition and its corrected Hom domains. Explicitly
the inverse of G(Y)[1]->G(Y[1]) is G's exact-functor
comparison; the forward map is adjoint to

\[
 F(G(Y)[1])\xrightarrow{\xi_{G(Y)}}F(G(Y))[1]
                         \xrightarrow{\epsilon_Y[1]}Y[1].
\]

Its naturality and bijectivity are the Yoneda calculation in
that proof. Applying F to a cone of G(A)->G(B) and using
the counit gives the comparison cone->G(C); testing with
Hom_D(W,-), using adjunction and the two exact sequences,
makes that comparison an isomorphism. The domain is D,
not D', as reconciled there. Thus the already verified
exact-adjoint theorem applies without any new operation.

## 7. Geometric receiving category and three received reports

The citation in perfect.tex 4323–4326 must be read in its
actual category. Proposition compact-is-perfect at 4246–4251
concerns D_QCoh(O_X), for quasi-compact quasi-separated X.
Its proof at 4253–4265 uses

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{D(O_X)}(P,M)
    =H^0R\Gamma(X,P^\vee\otimes^{\mathbf L}M)
\]

and the preservation of coproducts by RΓ on D_QCoh.
The underlying morphisms may be computed in D(O_X) because
the inclusion is fully faithful. The compactness quantifier,
however, ranges over families in D_QCoh. It does not thereby
range over arbitrary objects of D(O_X).

The exact sufficient comparison is the inclusion

\[
 D_{\mathrm{QCoh},T}(O_X)
    \xrightarrow{\ j\ }D_{\mathrm{QCoh}}(O_X)
    \xrightarrow{\ k\ }D(O_X).
\]

Both are full triangulated inclusions and preserve the indicated
coproducts. For a perfect supported P and a family M_l in the
supported category, its compactness comparison there is,
under full faithfulness, precisely its comparison for the same
family in D_QCoh. Thus it is an isomorphism. This proves the
forward implication of compact-is-perfect-with-support in the
needed category; Section 4 supplies the categorical part of its
converse with the supported perfect generator.

This validates P05-EN-CH36-0050 / OCC-05095: replace the
ambient D(O_X) in line 4324 by D_QCoh(O_X). It is an

here. P05-EN-CH36-0051 / OCC-05096 is the distinct missing
