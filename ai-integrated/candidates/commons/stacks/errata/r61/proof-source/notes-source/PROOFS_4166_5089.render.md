## 1. Finite filtrations in each degree and the target of gr

In an abelian category A, an object of Fil^f(A) is an object M with a
decreasing filtration F^p M, equal to M for sufficiently small p and to
zero for sufficiently large p. Its p-th graded object is
F^p M/F^{p+1} M. Gr(A) is the category of families indexed by p, with
morphisms and abelian operations computed componentwise. In particular,
the notation gr(M) does not require a countable coproduct in A.

Each gr^p, gr, and the forgetful functor is additive. An additive functor
preserves the actual biproducts used in shifts and cones. For a map u of
cochain complexes, its cone in degree n is K^{n+1} direct-sum L^n with
differential (-d\_K,0;u,d\_L). Applying the functor gives precisely the
cone matrix for its image. The shift comparison is the componentwise
identity. Thus all three functors induce exact functors on homotopy
categories. The composition of gr with H^0 is homological because
cohomology of a cone gives the usual long exact sequence in Gr(A).
The same argument applies to gr^p and to forgetting F.

Consequently the complexes with H^n(gr K)=0 for every n form the
strictly full saturated triangulated kernel described by the previously
proved homological-kernel construction. A morphism is a filtered
quasi-isomorphism exactly when its cone belongs to that kernel: apply
the long exact graded cohomology sequence in every degree. This proves
the source's statements about FAc, FQis, the quotient, and the factorization
of H^0 gr. Kernels, cycles, and cohomology here are taken in Gr(A), which
is abelian; no abelian structure on Fil^f(A) is being presumed.

For completeness, the finite-filtration exactness argument used to
forget F is local in cochain degree. For three finite filtered objects
A0 -> A1 -> A2, exactness of every graded sequence implies exactness
of the underlying sequence and of the sequences of filtration steps
and filtration quotients. To prove this, choose a common finite
filtration interval for these three objects. Remove its highest nonzero
step. The highest-step complex is the corresponding graded complex,
and the quotient has a shorter filtration. Induction applies to the
quotient; the termwise short exact sequence from highest steps to the
original complex to the quotient then gives exactness at the middle.
That last assertion follows directly by lifting a middle cycle to the
left term of the quotient, subtracting its lifted boundary, and lifting
the resulting highest-step cycle. In an arbitrary abelian category
these lifts are obtained after epimorphic pullbacks; images descend
along these epimorphisms. The complete test-object argument is in
`../HOMOLOGY_INTAKE_20260930/PROOFS_4681_5648.md`, lines 265–281.
Applying the same induction to the induced step filtrations and
quotient filtrations gives their exactness as well.

## 6. The full domain, its localization, and the zero-object overclaim

Let E be the full subcategory where RF exists. The zero object is in E:
the object id\_0 of 0/S is terminal, and its image is F(0)=0. The
essential-constancy witness is 0 -> F(0); every index has its unique
map to this terminal object, giving the required eventual equality.
The dual initial object of S/0 proves the LF assertion as well.
Inverting isomorphisms, shifts, and section 4 prove E is strictly full
and triangulated. Sections 3–4 give the exact functor RF on it.

If an arrow of S has either endpoint in E, section 3 puts the other
endpoint in E. This property proves all the multiplicative-system
axioms for S\_E inside E: an MS2 construction starting with objects in
E has its new endpoint in E because the new comparison arrow is in S;
the same applies to an MS3 equalizing denominator. Identities,
composition and saturation restrict directly. Shifts preserve E;
in MS6 the third vertex belongs to E by its triangle, and the third
denominator has both endpoints there. Hence S\_E is compatible.

For X,Y in E a fraction X -> T <- Y in the larger localization
already has T in E, since the arrow out of Y is a denominator. The
same is true of every later denominator used to compare two such
fractions. Thus both the morphisms and their equality relation are
exactly those in S\_E^{-1}E. This proves full faithfulness, not just
faithfulness on original morphisms. Localized distinguished triangles
are images of triangles up to isomorphism, so this embedding is exact.
RF inverts S\_E and therefore factors with its specified shift comparison
as the stated exact functor on S\_E^{-1}E.

If D' is Karoubian, every e of section 5 splits, so E is closed under
existing summands of its objects. More precisely, the same conclusion
holds whenever just the idempotents RF(i\_Xp\_X) arising from decompositions
of objects in E split. Conversely closure under these summands forces
these idempotents to split by (5.2). This propagates DERIVED-UNDER-006
to part (8) of proposition-derived-functor without replacing that
proposition's original sufficient hypothesis.

## 7. Computing objects and the receiving variance correction

The canonical cocone map eta\_X:F(X) -> RF(X) is natural in X by
section 3 with the identity denominator. It commutes with the shift
comparisons because those comparisons are induced at every index.

distinguished triangle of defined objects, eta gives a morphism of

any pair of components. The LF argument uses the natural cone map
