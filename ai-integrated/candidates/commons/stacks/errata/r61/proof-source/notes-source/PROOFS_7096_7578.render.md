## 6. The equivalence and the meaning of uniqueness

The full subcategory of injectives is additive and closed
under finite sums. Shifts and mapping cones of bounded-below
complexes in it stay there. The canonical functor
q:K^+(I)->D^+(A) therefore preserves distinguished cone
triangles and shifts. Every object of D^+(A) has a bounded
below representative and an injective resolution, giving
essential surjectivity. For complexes U,V in K^+(I), the
injective Hom comparison proves
Hom\_{K(A)}(U,V)=Hom\_{D(A)}(U,V). The bounded homotopy and
derived subcategories are full on these objects, so this
is the required full faithfulness. This proves original
7377–7397 with its actual functor.
