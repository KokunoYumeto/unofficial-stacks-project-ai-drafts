# R48: Groupoids and Derived Categories of Spaces

This is an independently reviewed correction candidate for the Stacks Project
source pinned at commit `a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14`.
It contains 24 source-correction units and 27 exact edits: 19 Chapter 75
producer findings and five recovered groupoid findings. The stable identifiers
are `MC-STK-ERR-1545` through `MC-STK-ERR-1568`.

The [source map](source-map.jsonl) links each unit to its exact original text,
replacement, source coordinates and rationale. The independent source review
under `replay/review-inputs/independent_source_review_20260912/` records the mathematical reasoning, rejected
alternatives and comparison with earlier admitted corrections. Successful
byte replay alone is not a mathematical verdict.

## How to use this candidate

`payload/groupoids.tex` and `payload/spaces-perfect.tex` contain only this
round's changes against the pinned official source. They are **not cumulative
replacements** for an integrated edition. A composer must apply the admitted
operations in registry order, preserving earlier corrections and additions.
Candidate construction, independent validation, registry admission, cumulative
composition and public release are separate transitions.

`candidate.config.json` and its regeneration input are preserved source-stage
snapshots. Their `not_performed` fields describe the state when those bytes
were frozen. Later actual evidence is in `builds/`, `replay/`, and the final
candidate manifest when present. A construction lease is not admission.

## Validation and honest limits

The two standalone candidate chapters contain 55 and 75 pages. Candidate and
authority PDFs were each built twice, with identical PDF bytes between the two
runs. A single machine-wide TeX mutex covered the captured process trees and
their immediate checks. Independent source, build and visual reviews are
separately recorded; every page received a contact-sheet layout review and
every correction-sensitive page received an expanded review. This does not
claim exhaustive small-glyph proofreading of every unchanged page.

The standalone PDFs retain inherited external-reference placeholders and
layout warnings. Groupoids retains two overfull horizontal boxes and one
underfull vertical box; Derived Categories of Spaces retains twelve overfull
horizontal boxes and one overfull vertical box. Expanded visual inspection
found no blocking clipping or collision. These PDFs are untagged and are not
claimed to meet accessibility-conformance standards. Groupoids retains an
authority label-rerun warning; the fixed three-pass recipe does not prove AUX
convergence.

The build audit also preserves evidence-retention limits: the second run
overwrote the first execution JSON, although retained command outputs and the
guarded fail-fast execution support its success. Some generated AUX, OUT, TOC
and BBL bytes were not retained after the owned build directories were removed.
Their recorded input hashes agree between builds, but absent bytes cannot now
be independently rehashed. The candidate is not a self-contained archive of
the TeX toolchain or all transient build inputs.

Other chapter defects and later producer packets remain separate findings;
this finite review does not claim that the full chapters are error-free.
