## Alternatives and distinctions

The first-affine-member sentence in SD-003 need not be false; it is insufficient for the induction over every member. The correction's rationale must retain that qualification. In SD-007, deleting the adjective would be possible but less precise than the locally supported term “left exact”.

The zero-multiplication correction preserves the full iff theorem, including d=0. It is distinct from admitted MC-STK-ERR-1494, which corrects the earlier degree lemma's hypothesis. A zero-dimensional integral variety with its identity rational point would be Spec(k), so a nonzero abelian variety has positive dimension; the zero map's fibre over the identity is all of that variety and cannot be an étale fibre.

For the groupoid inverse correction, the source cocycle is pr_1*alpha composed with pr_0*alpha = c*alpha. Pullback by (i,1) yields alpha composed with i*alpha = s*e*alpha, and pullback by (1,i) yields i*alpha composed with alpha = t*e*alpha. Swapping only the two right-hand labels would produce true standalone identities assigned to the wrong named pullbacks. The selected two left-hand corrections repair the written derivation completely.
