# Fields: corrections and complete decomposition proof

Forty-seven proposed corrections and clarifications, consisting of 56 exact edits: 21 copyedits, 21 source corrections and five clarifications. These repair field and quotient domains, polynomial arguments, characteristic-zero cases and a dropped Kummer sign. They include a complete proof of the normal algebraic decomposition and expose the transcendence-basis extension property already proved in the source. All eight earlier Fields corrections remain intact. [Read each correction and its reason](REVIEW.md).

AI correction review, source preparation, builds and visual checks: OpenAI Codex — GPT-6 Astra, Ultra effort. These are unofficial suggestions with no human review or upstream endorsement. Admission and cumulative composition are later, separately recorded transitions.

The [complete normal-decomposition proof](evidence/FIELDS_NORMAL_DECOMPOSITION_PROOF_20260925.tex) and [primary proof check and further consequences](evidence/FIELDS_NORMAL_DECOMPOSITION_PROOF_CHECK_20260925.md) retain exact source locators. The latter proves linear disjointness without normality, preservation of both degrees, and the canonical topological Galois-group comparison. These consequences are separate from the received erratum; no novelty is claimed.
