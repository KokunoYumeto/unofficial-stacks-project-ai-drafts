9453: \label{definition-invertible}
9454: Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a monoidal category. An object $X$ of $\mathcal{C}$
9455: is called {\it invertible} if any (or all) of the equivalent conditions of

9464: \begin{definition}
9465: \label{definition-dual}
9466: Given a monoidal category $(\mathcal{C}, \otimes, \phi)$
9467: and an object $X$ a {\it left dual} is an object $Y$ together with
9468: morphisms $\eta : \mathbf{1} \to X \otimes Y$ and
9469: $\epsilon : Y \otimes X \to \mathbf{1}$
9470: such that the diagrams
9471: $$
9472: \vcenter{
9473: \xymatrix{
9474: X \ar[rd]_1 \ar[r]_-{\eta \otimes 1} &
9475: X \otimes Y \otimes X \ar[d]^{1 \otimes \epsilon} \\
9476: & X
9477: }
9478: }
9479: \quad\text{and}\quad
9480: \vcenter{
9481: \xymatrix{
9482: Y \ar[rd]_1 \ar[r]_-{1 \otimes \eta} &
9483: Y \otimes X \otimes Y \ar[d]^{\epsilon \otimes 1} \\
9484: & Y
9485: }
9486: }
9487: $$
9488: commute. In this situation we say that $X$ is a {\it right dual} of $Y$.
9489: \end{definition}
9490: 
9491: \noindent
9492: Observe that if $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$ is a functor of
9493: monoidal categories, then $F(Y)$ is a left dual of $F(X)$ if
9494: $Y$ is a left dual of $X$.

9496: \begin{lemma}
9497: \label{lemma-left-dual}
9498: Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a monoidal category. If $Y$ is a left dual to $X$,
9499: then
9500: $$
9501: \Mor(Z' \otimes X, Z) = \Mor(Z', Z \otimes Y)
9502: \quad\text{and}\quad
9503: \Mor(Y \otimes Z', Z) = \Mor(Z', X \otimes Z)
9504: $$
9505: functorially in $Z$ and $Z'$.
9506: \end{lemma}

9514: $$

9516: and the sequence of maps

9518: \Mor(Z', Z \otimes Y) \to
9519: \Mor(Z' \otimes X, Z \otimes Y \otimes X) \to
