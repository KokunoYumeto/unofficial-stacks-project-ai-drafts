5179: Conversely, assume that $RF$ is defined at $X \oplus Y$ and that $\mathcal{D}'$
5180: is Karoubian. Since $S$ is a saturated system $S$ is the set of arrows which
5181: become invertible under the additive localization functor
5182: $Q : \mathcal{D} \to S^{-1}\mathcal{D}$, see
5183: Categories, Lemma \ref{categories-lemma-what-gets-inverted}.
5184: Thus for any $s : X \to X'$ and $s' : Y \to Y'$ in $S$ the morphism
5185: $s \oplus s' : X \oplus Y \to X' \oplus Y'$ is an element of $S$.
5186: In this way we obtain a functor
5187: $$
5188: X/S \times Y/S \longrightarrow (X \oplus Y)/S
5189: $$
5190: Recall that the categories $X/S, Y/S, (X \oplus Y)/S$ are filtered
5191: (Categories, Remark
5192: \ref{categories-remark-left-localization-morphisms-colimit}).
5193: By Categories, Lemma \ref{categories-lemma-essentially-constant-over-product}
5194: $X/S \times Y/S$ is filtered and
5195: $F|_{X/S} : X/S \to \mathcal{D}'$ (resp.\ $F|_{Y/S} : Y/S \to \mathcal{D}'$)
5196: is essentially constant if and only if
5197: $F|_{X/S} \circ \text{pr}_1 : X/S \times Y/S \to \mathcal{D}'$
5198: (resp.\ $F|_{Y/S} \circ \text{pr}_2 : X/S \times Y/S \to \mathcal{D}'$)
5199: is essentially constant. Below we will show that the displayed functor
5200: is cofinal, hence by
5201: Categories, Lemma \ref{categories-lemma-cofinal-essentially-constant},
5202: we see that $F|_{(X \oplus Y)/S}$ is essentially constant implies that
5203: $F|_{X/S} \circ \text{pr}_1 \oplus F|_{Y/S} \circ \text{pr}_2 :
5204: X/S \times Y/S \to \mathcal{D}'$
5205: is essentially constant. By Homology, Lemma
5206: \ref{homology-lemma-direct-sum-from-product-essentially-constant}
5207: (and this is where we use that $\mathcal{D}'$ is Karoubian)
5208: we see that 
5209: $F|_{X/S} \circ \text{pr}_1 \oplus F|_{Y/S} \circ \text{pr}_2$
5210: being essentially constant implies
5211: $F|_{X/S} \circ \text{pr}_1$ and
5212: $F|_{Y/S} \circ \text{pr}_2$ are essentially constant proving that $RF$ is
5213: defined at $X$ and $Y$.
