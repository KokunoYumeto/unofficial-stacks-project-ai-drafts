2220: problem posed by the lemma. Namely, $F^pJ^n$ is an injective object
2221: of $\mathcal{A}$ because it is isomorphic to
2222: $I^{p, n} \times \prod_{p' > p} J^{p', n}$ and products of
2223: injectives are injective. Then the injective map $F^pJ^n \to J^n$
2224: splits and hence the quotient $J^n/F^pJ^n$ is injective as well
2225: as a direct summand of the injective object $J^n$.
2226: Similarly for $F^pJ^n/F^{p'}J^n$. This in particular means
2227: that $0 \to F^pJ^\bullet \to J^\bullet \to J^\bullet/F^pJ^\bullet \to 0$
2228: is a termwise split short exact sequence of complexes, hence defines
2229: a distinguished triangle in $K(\mathcal{A})$ by fiat.
2230: Since $J^\bullet$ and $F^pJ^\bullet$ are K-injective complexes
2231: we see that the same is true for $J^\bullet/F^pJ^\bullet$
2232: by Derived Categories, Lemma \ref{derived-lemma-triangle-K-injective}.
2233: A similar argument shows that $F^pJ^\bullet/F^{p'}J^\bullet$
2234: is K-injective. By construction $j : K^\bullet \to J^\bullet$
2235: and the induced maps $F^pK^\bullet \to F^pJ^\bullet$ are
2236: quasi-isomorphisms. Using the long exact cohomology sequences
2237: of the complexes in play we find that the same holds for
2238: $K^\bullet/F^pK^\bullet \to J^\bullet/F^pJ^\bullet$ and
2239: $F^pK^\bullet/F^{p'}K^\bullet \to F^pJ^\bullet/F^{p'}J^\bullet$.
2240: \end{proof}
