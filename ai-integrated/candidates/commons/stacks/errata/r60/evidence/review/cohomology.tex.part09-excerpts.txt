10705: \longrightarrow
10706: \Gamma(X, \mathcal{I}^\bullet) =
10707: \lim_n \Gamma(X, \mathcal{I}_n^\bullet)
10708: $$
10709: Here the arrow is in the derived category, but the equality on the
10710: right holds on the level of complexes.
10711: Note that (3)(b) shows that $\tau_{\geq -n}\mathcal{F}^\bullet$
10712: is a bounded below complex satisfying the hypothesis of the lemma.
10713: Thus the case of bounded below complexes shows each of the maps
10714: $$
10715: \text{Tot}(\check{\mathcal{C}}^\bullet_{alt}(\mathcal{U},
10716: \tau_{\geq -n}\mathcal{F}^\bullet))
10717: \longrightarrow
10718: \Gamma(X, \mathcal{I}_n^\bullet)
10719: $$
10720: is a quasi-isomorphism. The cohomologies of the complexes on the left
10721: hand side in given degree are eventually
10722: constant (as the alternating {\v C}ech complex is finite).
10723: Hence the same is true on the right hand side.
10724: Thus the cohomology of the limit on the right hand side is
10725: this constant value by
10726: Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-apply-Mittag-Leffler-again}
10727: (or the stronger More on Algebra, Lemma
10728: \ref{more-algebra-lemma-apply-Mittag-Leffler-again})
10729: and we win.
10730: \end{proof}
