3927: In case (2) there is a short exact sequence
3928: $$
3929: 0 \to \mathcal{K}_\beta \to \mathcal{K}_\alpha \to \mathcal{N}_\alpha \to 0
3930: $$
3931: By Remark \ref{remark-why-graded-injective}
3932: and since we've seen that $\mathcal{I}$ is graded
3933: injective, we obtain an exact sequence
3934: $$
3935: \Hom_{K(\textit{Mod}(\mathcal{A}, \text{d}))}(\mathcal{K}_\beta, \mathcal{I})
3936: \to
3937: \Hom_{K(\textit{Mod}(\mathcal{A}, \text{d}))}(\mathcal{K}_\alpha, \mathcal{I})
3938: \to
3939: \Hom_{K(\textit{Mod}(\mathcal{A}, \text{d}))}(\mathcal{N}_\alpha, \mathcal{I})
3940: $$
3941: By induction the term on the left is zero. By assumption (2)
3942: the term on the right is zero: any map $\mathcal{M}_s \to \mathcal{I}$
3943: factors through $\mathcal{M}'_s$ and hence is homotopic to zero.
3944: Thus the middle group is zero too. Finally, suppose that $\alpha$ is a
3945: limit ordinal. Because we also have
3946: $\mathcal{K}_\alpha = \colim \mathcal{K}_\alpha$ as graded
3947: $\mathcal{A}$-modules we see that
3948: $$
3949: \Hom_{\textit{Mod}^{dg}(\mathcal{A}, \text{d})}
3950: (\mathcal{K}_\alpha, \mathcal{I})
3951: = \lim_{\beta < \alpha}
3952: \Hom_{\textit{Mod}^{dg}(\mathcal{A}, \text{d})}
3953: (\mathcal{K}_\beta, \mathcal{I})
3954: $$
3955: as complexes of abelian groups. The cohomology groups of these
3956: complexes compute morphisms in $K(\textit{Mod}(\mathcal{A}, \text{d}))$
3957: between shifts. The transition maps in the system of complexes
3958: are surjective by Remark \ref{remark-why-graded-injective}
3959: because $\mathcal{I}$ is graded injective.
3960: Moreover, for a limit ordinal $\beta \leq \alpha$
3961: we have equality of limit and value. Thus we may apply
3962: Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-ML-over-ordinals}
3963: to conclude.
3964: \end{proof}
