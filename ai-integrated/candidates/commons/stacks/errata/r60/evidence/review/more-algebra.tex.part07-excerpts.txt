16126: \begin{lemma}
16127: \label{lemma-kunneth-converges}
16128: Under the assumptions above, if in addition
16129: \begin{enumerate}
16130: \item the filtration on $K^\bullet$ is finite and the filtration on
16131: $L^\bullet$ is finite, or
16132: \item more generally the following are true
16133: \begin{enumerate}
16134: \item $F^iK^\bullet$ is acyclic for $i \gg 0$,
16135: \item $F^iK^\bullet \to K^\bullet$ is a quasi-isomorphism for $i \ll 0$,
16136: \item $F^jL^\bullet$ is acyclic for $j \gg 0$, and
16137: \item $F^jL^\bullet \to L^\bullet$ is a quasi-isomorphism for $j \ll 0$.
16138: \end{enumerate}
16139: \end{enumerate}
16140: Then the spectral sequence is bounded, the associated filtration on each
16141: $H^n(T^\bullet) = H^n(K^\bullet \otimes_R^\mathbf{L} L^\bullet)$ is finite
16142: and we have convergence
16143: $$
16144: E_1^{p, q} =
16145: \bigoplus\nolimits_{i + j = p}
16146: H^{p + q}(\text{gr}^iK^\bullet \otimes_R^\mathbf{L} \text{gr}^jL^\bullet)
16147: \Rightarrow
16148: H^n(K^\bullet \otimes_R^\mathbf{L} L^\bullet)
16149: $$
16150: \end{lemma}
16151: 
16152: \begin{proof}
16153: In case (1) the filtration on $T^\bullet$ is finite and the lemma follows
16154: immediately from
16155: Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-biregular-ss-converges}.
16156: In case (2) choose $a < b$ such that $F^iK^\bullet$ and $F^jL^\bullet$ are
16157: acyclic for $i, j > b$ and $F^iK^\bullet \to K^\bullet$ and
16158: $F^jL^\bullet \to L^\bullet$ are quasi-isomorphisms for $i, j < a$.
16159: We claim that in this case the complex $F^nT^\bullet$ is acyclic for
16160: $n > 2b$ and that $F^nT^\bullet \to T^\bullet$ is a quasi-isomorphism
16161: for $n < 2a - 1$. Since the claim shows that
16162: Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-ss-converges-trivial}
16163: applies we see that our lemma is true.
16164: 
16165: \medskip\noindent
16166: Proof of the claim. Given $n$ and integers $i_1 \leq i_2$ consider
16167: $$
16168: S^{n, i_1, i_2} =
16169: \Im\left(
16170: \bigoplus\nolimits_{i_1 \leq i \leq i_2}
16171: \text{Tot}(F^iK^\bullet \otimes_R F^{n - i}L^\bullet)
16172: \to
16173: \text{Tot}(K^\bullet \otimes_R L^\bullet)
16174: \right)
16175: $$
16176: viewed as a subcomplex of $\text{Tot}(K^\bullet \otimes_R L^\bullet)$.
16177: Observe that $F^nT^\bullet = \colim S^{n, i_1, i_2}$ is a filtered colimit.
16178: Thus to show the claim it suffices to show that $S^{n, i_1, i_2}$ is acyclic
16179: for $n > 2b$ and that
16180: $S^{n, i_1, i_2} \to \text{Tot}(K^\bullet \otimes L^\bullet)$
16181: is a quasi-isomorphism for $n < 2a - 1$ and a cofinal set of choices of
16182: pairs $i_1, i_2$.
16183: 
16184: \medskip\noindent
16185: If $i_1 = i_2 = i$ and $n > 2b$, then
16186: $$
16187: S^{n, i, i} =
16188: \text{Tot}(F^iK^\bullet \otimes_R F^{n - i}L^\bullet)
16189: $$
16190: is acyclic: either $i > b$ and this complex is acyclic by 
16191: our assumption that $F^{n - i}L^\bullet$ is K-flat, or
16192: $i \leq b$ and then $n - i \geq n - b > b$ and the same holds.
16193: Using our flatness assumptions the reader shows that there is a short
16194: exact sequence
16195: $$
16196: 0 \to
16197: S^{n + 1, i_2 + 1, i_2 + 1}
16198: \to
16199: S^{n, i_1, i_2} \oplus S^{n, i_2 + 1, i_2 + 1}
16200: \to
16201: S^{n, i_1, i_2 + 1}
16202: \to
16203: 0
16204: $$
16205: of complexes. Thus we see by induction on $i_2 - i_1$ that all of the complexes
16206: $S^{n, i_1, i_2}$ for $n > 2b$ are acyclic.
16207: 
16208: \medskip\noindent
16209: Assume $n < 2a - 1$. Observe that
16210: $$
16211: S^{n, a - 1, a - 1} =
16212: \text{Tot}(F^{a - 1}K^\bullet \otimes_R F^{n - a + 1}L^\bullet)
16213: \to \text{Tot}(K^\bullet \otimes_R L^\bullet)
16214: $$
16215: is a quasi-isomorphism because both $a - 1 < a$ and $n - a + 1 < a$
16216: and all our complexes are K-flat hence both sides compute the
16217: same object of $D(R)$.
16218: Observe that for $i_2 \geq a - 1$ and $n < 2a - 1$ the map
16219: $$
16220: S^{n + 1, i_2 + 1, i_2 + 1} \to S^{n, i_2 + 1, i_2 + 1}
16221: $$
16222: is a quasi-isomorphism because left and right hand side are quasi-isomorphic
16223: to $\text{Tot}(F^{i_2 + 1}K^\bullet \otimes L^\bullet)$.
16224: Thus using the short exact
16225: sequence of complexes in the previous paragraph we find that
16226: $S^{n, a - 1, a - 1} \to S^{n, a - 1, t}$ is a quasi-isomorphism for
16227: all $t \geq a - 1$. On the other hand, there are similarly short
16228: exact sequences
16229: $$
16230: 0 \to
16231: S^{n + 1, i_1, i_1}
16232: \to
16233: S^{n, i_1 - 1, i_1 - 1} \oplus
16234: S^{n, i_1, i_2}
16235: \to
16236: S^{n, i_1 - 1, i_2}
16237: \to
16238: 0
16239: $$
16240: of complexes. Similarly to the above, we note that for $i_1 \leq a - 1$
16241: the map
16242: $$
16243: S^{n + 1, i_1, i_1} \to S^{n, i_1 - 1, i_1 - 1}
16244: $$
16245: is a quasi-isomorphism because left and right hand side are quasi-isomorphic
16246: to $\text{Tot}(K^\bullet \otimes F^{n - i_1 + 1}L^\bullet)$.
16247: Thus we conclude that $S^{n, s, t} \to S^{n, a - 1, t}$ is a quasi-isomorphism
16248: for all $s \leq a - 1$. Combined we find that $S^{n, s, t}$ for $s \leq a - 1$
16249: and $t \geq a - 1$ maps quasi-isomorphically to
16250: $\text{Tot}(K^\bullet \otimes_R L^\bullet)$.
16251: This finishes the proof of the lemma.
16252: \end{proof}

16409: \begin{lemma}[K\"unneth Spectral Sequence]
16410: \label{lemma-kunneth-spectral-sequence}
16411: Let $R$ be a ring. Let $K$ and $L$ be objects of $D^b(R)$.
16412: There exists a bigraded bounded spectral sequence $\{E_r\}_{r \geq 2}$
16413: with
16414: $$
16415: E_2^{p, q} = \bigoplus\nolimits_{i + j = q} \text{Tor}^R_{-p}(H^i(K), H^j(L))
16416: $$
16417: and $d_r$ of bidegree $(r, -r + 1)$
16418: converging to $H^{p + q}(K \otimes_R^\mathbf{L} L)$.
16419: \end{lemma}
16420: 
16421: \begin{proof}
16422: Let $K^\bullet$ be a complex of $R$-modules representing $K$ and let
16423: $L^\bullet$ be a complex of $R$-modules representing $L$. Set
16424: $F^iK^\bullet = \tau_{\leq -i}K^\bullet$ and similarly for $L$, see
16425: Homology, Section \ref{homology-section-truncations}. Apply
16426: Proposition \ref{proposition-kunneth-filtered} noting that
16427: $\text{gr}^iK^\bullet = H^{-i}(K)[i]$ to get a bounded
16428: spectral sequence $\{(E')_r\}_{r \geq 1}$ with
16429: $$
16430: (E')^{p, q}_1 =
16431: \bigoplus\nolimits_{i + j = p} \text{Tor}^R_{-2p - q}(H^{-i}(K), H^{-j}(L))
16432: $$
16433: converging to the cohomology of $K \otimes_R^\mathbf{L} L$. By
16434: construction this spectral sequence has differentials
16435: $d_r^{p, q} : (E')_r^{p, q} \to (E')_r^{p + r, q - r + 1}$ for $r \geq 1$.
16436: To get the spectral sequence of the lemma we set for $r \geq 2$
16437: $$
16438: E_r^{p, q} = (E')_{r - 1}^{-q, p + 2q}
16439: $$
16440: We leave it to the reader to show that this works.
16441: \end{proof}
