3951: \section{Derived categories}
3952: \label{section-derived-categories}
3953: 
3954: \noindent
3955: In this section we construct the derived category of an abelian category
3956: $\mathcal{A}$ by inverting the quasi-isomorphisms in $K(\mathcal{A})$.
3957: Before we do this recall that the functors
3958: $H^i : \text{Comp}(\mathcal{A}) \to \mathcal{A}$
3959: factor through $K(\mathcal{A})$, see
3960: Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-map-cohomology-homotopy-cochain}.
3961: Moreover, in
3962: Homology, Definition \ref{homology-definition-cohomology-shift}
3963: we have defined identifications $H^i(K^\bullet[n]) = H^{i + n}(K^\bullet)$.
3964: At this point it makes sense to redefine
3965: $$
3966: H^i(K^\bullet) = H^0(K^\bullet[i])
3967: $$
3968: in order to avoid confusion and possible sign errors.

3970: \begin{lemma}
3971: \label{lemma-cohomology-homological}
3972: Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. The functor
3973: $$
3974: H^0 : K(\mathcal{A}) \longrightarrow \mathcal{A}
3975: $$
3976: is homological.
3977: \end{lemma}
3978: 
3979: \begin{proof}
3980: Because $H^0$ is a functor, and by our definition of distinguished triangles
3981: it suffices to prove that given a termwise split short exact sequence
3982: of complexes $0 \to A^\bullet \to B^\bullet \to C^\bullet \to 0$
3983: the sequence $H^0(A^\bullet) \to H^0(B^\bullet) \to H^0(C^\bullet)$
3984: is exact. This follows from
3985: Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-long-exact-sequence-cochain}.
3986: \end{proof}
