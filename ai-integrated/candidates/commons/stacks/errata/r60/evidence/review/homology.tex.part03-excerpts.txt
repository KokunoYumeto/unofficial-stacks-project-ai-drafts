5585: For a differential object $(A, d)$ we denote
5586: $$
5587: H(A, d) = \Ker(d)/\Im(d)
5588: $$
5589: its {\it homology}.
5590: \end{definition}
5591: 
5592: \begin{lemma}
5593: \label{lemma-differential-objects-ses}
5594: Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category.
5595: Let $0 \to (A, d) \to (B, d) \to (C, d) \to 0$ be a short exact sequence
5596: of differential objects. Then we get an exact homology sequence
5597: $$
5598: \ldots \to H(C, d) \to H(A, d) \to H(B, d) \to H(C, d) \to \ldots
5599: $$
5600: \end{lemma}
5601: 
5602: \begin{proof}
5603: Apply Lemma \ref{lemma-long-exact-sequence-cochain}
5604: to the short exact sequence of complexes
5605: $$
5606: \begin{matrix}
5607: 0 & \to & A & \to & B & \to & C & \to & 0 \\
5608: & & \downarrow & & \downarrow & & \downarrow \\
5609: 0 & \to & A & \to & B & \to & C & \to & 0 \\
5610: & & \downarrow & & \downarrow & & \downarrow \\
5611: 0 & \to & A & \to & B & \to & C & \to & 0
5612: \end{matrix}
5613: $$
5614: where the vertical arrows are $d$.
5615: \end{proof}
