7240: K \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X}^\mathbf{L} L$ for which we can take the
7241: composition of the isomorphism
7242: $Lf^*(Rf_*K \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_Y}^\mathbf{L} Rf_*L) =
7243: Lf^*Rf_*K \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X}^\mathbf{L} Lf^*Rf_*L$
7244: (Lemma \ref{lemma-pullback-tensor-product})
7245: with the map
7246: $Lf^*Rf_*K \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X}^\mathbf{L} Lf^*Rf_*L

7249: \end{remark}

7256: \section{Cohomology of filtered complexes}
7257: \label{section-cohomology-filtered-object}
7258: 
7259: \noindent
7260: Filtered complexes of sheaves frequently come up in a natural fashion
7261: when studying cohomology of algebraic varieties, for example the de Rham
7262: complex comes with its Hodge filtration. In this sectionwe use the very general
7263: Injectives, Lemma \ref{injectives-lemma-K-injective-embedding-filtration}
7264: to find construct spectral sequences on cohomology and we relate these to
7265: previously constructed spectral sequences.
7266: 
7267: \begin{lemma}
7268: \label{lemma-spectral-sequence-filtered-object}
7269: Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_X)$ be a ringed space. Let $\mathcal{F}^\bullet$ be a
7270: filtered complex of $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules. There exists a canonical
7271: spectral sequence $(E_r, \text{d}_r)_{r \geq 1}$ of bigraded
7272: $\Gamma(X, \mathcal{O}_X)$-modules with $d_r$ of bidegree $(r, -r + 1)$ and
7273: $$
7274: E_1^{p, q} = H^{p + q}(X, \text{gr}^p\mathcal{F}^\bullet)
7275: $$
7276: If for every $n$ we have
7277: $$
7278: H^n(X, F^p\mathcal{F}^\bullet) = 0\text{ for }p \gg 0
7279: \quad\text{and}\quad
7280: H^n(X, F^p\mathcal{F}^\bullet) = H^n(X, \mathcal{F}^\bullet)\text{ for }p \ll 0
7281: $$
7282: then the spectral sequence is bounded and converges to
7283: $H^*(X, \mathcal{F}^\bullet)$.
7284: \end{lemma}
7285: 
7286: \begin{proof}
7287: (For a proof in case the complex is a bounded below complex
7288: of modules with finite filtrations, see the remark below.)
7289: Choose an map of filtered complexes
7290: $j : \mathcal{F}^\bullet \to \mathcal{J}^\bullet$ as in
7291: Injectives, Lemma
7292: \ref{injectives-lemma-K-injective-embedding-filtration}.
7293: The spectral sequence is the spectral sequence of
7294: Homology, Section \ref{homology-section-filtered-complex}
7295: associated to the filtered complex
7296: $$
7297: \Gamma(X, \mathcal{J}^\bullet)
7298: \quad\text{with}\quad
7299: F^p\Gamma(X, \mathcal{J}^\bullet) = \Gamma(X, F^p\mathcal{J}^\bullet)
7300: $$
7301: Since cohomology is computed by evaluating on K-injective representatives
7302: we see that the $E_1$ page is as stated in the lemma.
7303: The convergence and boundedness under the stated conditions
7304: follows from Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-ss-converges-trivial}.
7305: \end{proof}
