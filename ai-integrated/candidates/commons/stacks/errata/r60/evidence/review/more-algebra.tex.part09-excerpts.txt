6478: \begin{lemma}
6479: \label{lemma-limit-flat}
6480: Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring. Let $I$ be an ideal of $A$. Let
6481: $(M_n)$ be an inverse system of $A$-modules such that
6482: \begin{enumerate}
6483: \item $M_n$ is a flat $A/I^n$-module,
6484: \item $M_{n + 1} \to M_n$ is surjective.
6485: \end{enumerate}
6486: Then $M = \lim M_n$ is a flat $A$-module and
6487: $Q \otimes_A M = \lim Q \otimes_A M_n$ for every finite $A$-module $Q$.
6488: \end{lemma}
6489: 
6490: \begin{proof}
6491: We first show that $Q \otimes_A M = \lim Q \otimes_A M_n$ for every finite
6492: $A$-module $Q$. Choose a resolution $F_2 \to F_1 \to F_0 \to Q \to 0$
6493: by finite free $A$-modules $F_i$. Then
6494: $$
6495: F_2 \otimes_A M_n \to F_1 \otimes_A M_n \to F_0 \otimes_A M_n
6496: $$
6497: is a chain complex whose homology in degree $0$ is $Q \otimes_A M_n$
6498: and whose homology in degree $1$ is
6499: $$
6500: \text{Tor}_1^A(Q, M_n) = \text{Tor}_1^A(Q, A/I^n) \otimes_{A/I^n} M_n
6501: $$
6502: as $M_n$ is flat over $A/I^n$. By Lemma \ref{lemma-tor-strictly-pro-zero}
6503: we see that this system is essentially constant (with value $0$).
6504: It follows from Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-apply-Mittag-Leffler-again}
6505: that $\lim Q \otimes_A M_n =
6506: \Coker(\lim F_1 \otimes_A M_n \to \lim F_0 \otimes_A M_n)$.
6507: Since $F_i$ is finite free this equals
6508: $\Coker(F_1 \otimes_A M \to F_0 \otimes_A M) = Q \otimes_A M$.
6509: 
6510: \medskip\noindent
6511: Next, let $Q \to Q'$ be an injective map of finite $A$-modules.
6512: We have to show that $Q \otimes_A M \to Q' \otimes_A M$ is injective
6513: (Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-flat}). By the above we see
6514: $$
6515: \Ker(Q \otimes_A M \to Q' \otimes_A M) =
6516: \Ker(\lim Q \otimes_A M_n \to \lim Q' \otimes_A M_n).
6517: $$
6518: For each $n$ we have an exact sequence
6519: $$
6520: \text{Tor}_1^A(Q', M_n) \to \text{Tor}_1^A(Q'', M_n) \to
6521: Q \otimes_A M_n \to Q' \otimes_A M_n
6522: $$
6523: where $Q'' = \Coker(Q \to Q')$. Above we have seen that the
6524: inverse systems of Tor's are essentially constant with value $0$.
6525: It follows from
6526: Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-apply-Mittag-Leffler-again}
6527: that the inverse limit of the right most maps is injective.
6528: \end{proof}
