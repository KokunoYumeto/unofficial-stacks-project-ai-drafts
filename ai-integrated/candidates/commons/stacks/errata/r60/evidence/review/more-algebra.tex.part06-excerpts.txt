16065: of $K^n/F^{i + 1}K^n \otimes_R L^m/F^{j + 1}L^m$.
16066: Consider the map
16067: $$
16068: F^nT^n \longrightarrow
16069: \bigoplus\nolimits_{i + j = n}
16070: \text{Tot}(K^\bullet / F^{i + 1}K^\bullet
16071: \otimes_R L^\bullet / F^{j + 1}L^\bullet)
16072: $$
16073: We leave it to the reader to show that we do indeed end up in the direct
16074: sum and not the direct product, due to our definition of $F^nT^\bullet$.
16075: For $a + b = n$, the restriction of the displayed arrow to the subcomplex
16076: $\text{Tot}(F^aK^\bullet \otimes_R F^bL^\bullet)$ of $F^nT^\bullet$
16077: maps into the summand with $i = a$ and $j = b$.
16078: Moreover, by our flatness assumptions, the image is isomorphic to
16079: $\text{Tot}(\text{gr}^iK^\bullet \otimes_R \text{gr}^jL^\bullet)$
16080: and the kernel of $\text{Tot}(F^iK^\bullet \otimes_R F^jL^\bullet) \to
16081: \text{Tot}(\text{gr}^iK^\bullet \otimes_R \text{gr}^jL^\bullet)$ is
16082: $\text{Tot}(F^{i + 1}K^\bullet \otimes_R F^jL^\bullet) +
16083: \text{Tot}(F^iK^\bullet \otimes_R F^{j + 1}L^\bullet)$.
16084: It follows that we have a short exact sequence of complexes
16085: $$
16086: 0 \to F^{n + 1}T^\bullet \to F^nT^\bullet \to
16087: \bigoplus\nolimits_{i + j = n}
16088: \text{Tot}(\text{gr}^iK^\bullet \otimes_R \text{gr}^jL^\bullet) \to 0
16089: $$
16090: under our assumptions. In other words, this tells us that
16091: $\text{gr}^nT^\bullet$ is a direct sum of the complexes
16092: $\text{Tot}(\text{gr}^iK^\bullet \otimes_R \text{gr}^jL^\bullet)$
16093: for $i + j = n$.

16111: \text{gr}^nT^\bullet \to 0
16112: $$
16113: as explained in Homology, Lemma
16114: \ref{homology-lemma-spectral-sequence-filtered-complex-d1}.
16115: In particular, the reader can show this means that the summand

16118: $$

16120: \text{gr}^jL^\bullet) \oplus
16121: H^{p + q + 1}(\text{gr}^iK^\bullet \otimes_R^\mathbf{L}
16122: \text{gr}^{j + 1}L^\bullet)
16123: $$
16124: inside $E_1^{p + 1, q}$.

16126: \begin{lemma}
16127: \label{lemma-kunneth-converges}
16128: Under the assumptions above, if in addition
16129: \begin{enumerate}
16130: \item the filtration on $K^\bullet$ is finite and the filtration on
16131: $L^\bullet$ is finite, or
16132: \item more generally the following are true
16133: \begin{enumerate}
16134: \item $F^iK^\bullet$ is acyclic for $i \gg 0$,
16135: \item $F^iK^\bullet \to K^\bullet$ is a quasi-isomorphism for $i \ll 0$,
16136: \item $F^jL^\bullet$ is acyclic for $j \gg 0$, and
16137: \item $F^jL^\bullet \to L^\bullet$ is a quasi-isomorphism for $j \ll 0$.
16138: \end{enumerate}
16139: \end{enumerate}

16142: and we have convergence
