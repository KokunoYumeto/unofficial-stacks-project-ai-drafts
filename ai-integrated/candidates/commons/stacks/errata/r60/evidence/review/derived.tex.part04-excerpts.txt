3828: we see that $(A^\bullet, W^\bullet[-1], C^\bullet, -i[-1], p[-1], c)$
3829: is a distinguished triangle. By TR3 there is a morphism of distinguished
3830: triangles
3831: $(\text{id}, \beta, \text{id}) : (A^\bullet, B^\bullet, C^\bullet, a, b, c) \to
3832: (A^\bullet, W^\bullet[-1], C^\bullet, -i[-1], p[-1], c)$
3833: which must be an isomorphism by Lemma \ref{lemma-third-isomorphism-triangle}.
3834: This finishes the proof because
3835: $0 \to A^\bullet \to W^\bullet[-1] \to C^\bullet \to 0$
3836: is a termwise split short exact sequence of complexes
3837: by the very construction of cones in Section \ref{section-cones}.
3838: \end{proof}

3840: \begin{remark}
3841: \label{remark-homotopy-double}
3842: Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category with countable direct sums.
3843: Let $\text{DoubleComp}(\mathcal{A})$ denote the category of double complexes
3844: in $\mathcal{A}$, see
3845: Homology, Section \ref{homology-section-double-complexes}.
3846: We can use this category to construct two triangulated categories.
3847: \begin{enumerate}
3848: \item We can consider an object $A^{\bullet, \bullet}$ of
3849: $\text{DoubleComp}(\mathcal{A})$ as a complex of complexes
3850: as follows
3851: $$
3852: \ldots \to A^{\bullet, -1} \to A^{\bullet, 0} \to A^{\bullet, 1} \to \ldots
3853: $$
3854: and take the homotopy category $K_{first}(\text{DoubleComp}(\mathcal{A}))$
3855: with the corresponding triangulated structure given by
3856: Proposition \ref{proposition-homotopy-category-triangulated}.
3857: By Homology, Remark
3858: \ref{homology-remark-double-complex-complex-of-complexes-first} the functor
3859: $$
3860: \text{Tot} :
3861: K_{first}(\text{DoubleComp}(\mathcal{A}))
3862: \longrightarrow
3863: K(\mathcal{A})
3864: $$
3865: is an exact functor of triangulated categories.
3866: \item We can consider an object $A^{\bullet, \bullet}$ of
3867: $\text{DoubleComp}(\mathcal{A})$ as a complex of complexes
3868: as follows
3869: $$
3870: \ldots \to A^{-1, \bullet} \to A^{0, \bullet} \to A^{1, \bullet} \to \ldots
3871: $$
3872: and take the homotopy category $K_{second}(\text{DoubleComp}(\mathcal{A}))$
3873: with the corresponding triangulated structure given by
3874: Proposition \ref{proposition-homotopy-category-triangulated}.
3875: By Homology, Remark
3876: \ref{homology-remark-double-complex-complex-of-complexes-second} the functor
3877: $$
3878: \text{Tot} :
3879: K_{second}(\text{DoubleComp}(\mathcal{A}))
3880: \longrightarrow
3881: K(\mathcal{A})
3882: $$
3883: is an exact functor of triangulated categories.
3884: \end{enumerate}
3885: \end{remark}
