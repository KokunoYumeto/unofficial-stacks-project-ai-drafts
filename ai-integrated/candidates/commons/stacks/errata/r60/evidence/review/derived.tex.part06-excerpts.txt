8875: $E_1^{p, q} = R^{p + q}T(\text{gr}^p(K^\bullet))$.
8876: 
8877: \medskip\noindent
8878: On the other hand, each $I^n$ has a finite filtration and hence
8879: each $T(I^n)$ has a finite filtration. Thus we may apply
8880: Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-biregular-ss-converges}
8881: to conclude that the spectral sequence is bounded, converges to
8882: $H^*(T(I^\bullet)) = R^*T(K^\bullet)$
8883: and induces a finite filtration on each $H^n(T(I^\bullet)) = R^nT(K^\bullet)$.
8884: 
8885: \medskip\noindent
8886: Suppose that $K^\bullet \to L^\bullet$ is a morphism of
8887: $\text{Comp}^{+}(\text{Fil}^f(\mathcal{A}))$.
8888: Choose a filtered quasi-isomorphism $L^\bullet \to J^\bullet$
8889: with $J^\bullet$ a bounded below complex of filtered injective
8890: objects, see
8891: Lemma \ref{lemma-right-resolution-by-filtered-injectives}.
8892: By our results above,
8893: for example
8894: Lemma \ref{lemma-morphisms-into-filtered-injective-complex},
8895: there exists a diagram
8896: $$
8897: \xymatrix{
8898: K^\bullet \ar[r] \ar[d] & L^\bullet \ar[d] \\
8899: I^\bullet \ar[r] & J^\bullet
8900: }
8901: $$
8902: which commutes up to homotopy. Hence we get a morphism of filtered
8903: complexes $T(I^\bullet) \to T(J^\bullet)$ which gives rise to the
8904: morphism of spectral sequences, see
8905: Homology,
8906: Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-spectral-sequence-filtered-complex-functorial}.
8907: The last statement follows from this.
8908: \end{proof}
8909: 
8910: \begin{remark}
8911: \label{remark-final-functorial}
8912: As promised in Remark \ref{remark-functorial-ss} we discuss the connection
8913: of the lemma above with the constructions using Cartan-Eilenberg resolutions.
8914: Namely, let $T : \mathcal{A} \to \mathcal{B}$ be a left exact functor
8915: of abelian categories, assume $\mathcal{A}$
8916: has enough injectives, and let $K^\bullet$ be a bounded below complex
8917: of $\mathcal{A}$. We give an alternative construction of the
8918: spectral sequences ${}'E$ and ${}''E$ of
8919: Lemma \ref{lemma-two-ss-complex-functor}.
8920: 
8921: \medskip\noindent
8922: First spectral sequence. Consider the ``stupid'' filtration on $K^\bullet$
8923: obtained by setting $F^p(K^\bullet) = \sigma_{\geq p}(K^\bullet)$, see
8924: Homology, Section \ref{homology-section-truncations}.
8925: Note that this is stupid in the sense that
8926: $d(F^p(K^\bullet)) \subset F^{p + 1}(K^\bullet)$, compare
8927: Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-spectral-sequence-filtered-complex-d1}.
8928: Note that $\text{gr}^p(K^\bullet) = K^p[-p]$ with this filtration.
8929: According to Lemma \ref{lemma-ss-filtered-derived} there is a spectral sequence
8930: with $E_1$ term
8931: $$
8932: E_1^{p, q} = R^{p + q}T(K^p[-p]) = R^qT(K^p)
8933: $$
8934: as in the spectral sequence ${}'E_r$. Observe moreover that the differentials
8935: $E_1^{p, q} \to E_1^{p + 1, q}$ agree with the differentials in $'{}E_1$, see
8936: Homology, Lemma
8937: \ref{homology-lemma-spectral-sequence-filtered-complex-d1} part (2)
8938: and the description of ${}'d_1$ in the proof of
8939: Lemma \ref{lemma-two-ss-complex-functor}.
8940: 
8941: \medskip\noindent
8942: Second spectral sequence. Consider the filtration on the complex $K^\bullet$
8943: obtained by setting $F^p(K^\bullet) = \tau_{\leq -p}(K^\bullet)$, see
8944: Homology, Section \ref{homology-section-truncations}.
8945: The minus sign is necessary
8946: to get a decreasing filtration. Note that
8947: $\text{gr}^p(K^\bullet)$ is quasi-isomorphic to $H^{-p}(K^\bullet)[p]$
8948: with this filtration. According to Lemma \ref{lemma-ss-filtered-derived}
8949: there is a spectral sequence with $E_1$ term
8950: $$
8951: E_1^{p, q} = R^{p + q}T(H^{-p}(K^\bullet)[p])
8952: = R^{2p + q}T(H^{-p}(K^\bullet)) = {}''E_2^{i, j}
8953: $$
8954: with $i = 2p + q$ and $j = -p$. (This looks unnatural, but note that we
8955: could just as well have developed the whole theory of filtered complexes
8956: using increasing filtrations, with the end result that this then looks
8957: natural, but the other one doesn't.) We leave it to the reader to see
8958: that the differentials match up.
8959: 
8960: \medskip\noindent
8961: Actually, given a Cartan-Eilenberg resolution
8962: $K^\bullet \to I^{\bullet, \bullet}$ the induced morphism
8963: $K^\bullet \to \text{Tot}(I^{\bullet, \bullet})$
8964: into the associated total complex
8965: will be a filtered injective resolution for either filtration
8966: using suitable filtrations on $\text{Tot}(I^{\bullet, \bullet})$.
8967: This can be used
8968: to match up the spectral sequences exactly.
8969: \end{remark}
