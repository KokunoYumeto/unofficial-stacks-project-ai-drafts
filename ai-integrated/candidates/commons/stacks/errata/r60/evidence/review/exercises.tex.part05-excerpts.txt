2402: \noindent
2403: Note that given a morphism $\alpha : A \to B$ of filtered objects
2404: with finite filtrations to say that $\text{gr}(\alpha)$ injective
2405: is the same thing as saying that $\alpha$ is a {\it strict monomorphism}
2406: in the category $\text{Fil}(\mathcal{A})$. Namely,
2407: being a monomorphism means $\Ker(\alpha) = 0$ and strict means that
2408: this also implies $\Ker(\text{gr}(\alpha)) = 0$.
2409: See Homology, Lemma \ref{homology-lemma-characterize-strict}.

2411: although it makes sense in any additive category having kernels.)
