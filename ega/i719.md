## Independently written derivations

### 7.1.4: morphism germs

An irreducible scheme has a unique generic point eta by 01IS. By the definition
004X, every nonempty open contains eta: otherwise its closed complement
contains eta and thus its closure X. Conversely an open containing eta is
dense. The domains and equality witnesses of 01RS are therefore exactly those
in the stalk construction 0078 for the presheaf U -> Mor(U,Y). The two quotients
are canonically identical. Replacing Mor by Mor_S gives the relative case.
This argument needs no reducedness, separatedness, or finiteness of Y.
It concerns germs of morphisms, not arbitrary maps of generic local rings.

### 7.1.5: all assertions, the unwrapped proof, and the integral tail

The preceding argument applied to the structure sheaf gives
R(X) = O_(X,eta). Alternatively specialize the finite-component product
formula 01RV to one component; it genuinely generalizes this first assertion.
The earlier S000122 already uses that generic-stalk result, so no duplicate
root lemma is introduced.

Choose a nonempty affine neighbourhood Spec(A) of eta. By 00ES its generic
prime p is the unique minimal prime, hence the nilradical. By 01HV(6),
O_(X,eta) = A_p. By 00EU every element of its maximal ideal is nilpotent.
Every prime contains all nilpotents and lies inside that maximal ideal, so
the maximal ideal is its only prime and its dimension is zero.

If X is Noetherian, 01OW gives a Noetherian affine ring A, 00FN gives
Noetherian A_p, and 00KH makes it Artinian. Locally Noetherian already suffices
here, but the source hypothesis is retained. The source proof additionally
asserts nilpotence of p in A itself (French LF102). Write
p = (a_1,...,a_r), choose n_i >= 1 with a_i^(n_i) = 0, and set
N = 1 + sum_i(n_i - 1). Every degree-N monomial in the generators has an
exponent at least n_i, so p^N = 0. If p = 0 this is immediate. This proves
the proof-owned sentence, not merely its localized Artinian consequence.

For integral X the affine ring A is a domain, p = 0, and 01HV(6) plus 09FJ
identify A_(0) with Frac(A). The field assertion is also explicit in 01RV:
this is same-hypothesis coverage, not stronger coverage of all of 7.1.5.
Only when X is affine does this say R(X) = Frac(Gamma(X,O_X)).

For every z in an integral X, choose an affine neighbourhood Spec(A), with
z corresponding to q. It contains eta. Then
O_(X,z) = A_q injects into Frac(A) = R(X); injectivity follows because A is
a domain. Iterated localization 00C9 gives Frac(A_q) = Frac(A).
Intrinsically, send a germ represented near z to the generic germ of that
section. Equal representatives agree on a neighbourhood of z, which is
dense, so this is independent of the representative and of the affine
choice. It is the unique rational class represented by such a germ at z.
This accounts for all of the source's local-stalk tail without separation.

### 7.1.6–7: the finite product of morphism germs

Let X_1,...,X_n be the distinct irreducible components, with generic points
eta_i. Put V_i = X minus the union of the X_j with j different from i.
Finiteness makes V_i open; it contains eta_i, lies in X_i, and is irreducible.
The V_i are pairwise disjoint and their union is dense. A dense open U meets
each nonempty open V_i, and U intersect V_i contains eta_i. Conversely, an
open containing every eta_i is dense.

The component-removal and product paragraph in 0BX8, LF12641–12651, supplies
unlabelled proof coverage of this reduction; its whole statement compares
different objects and is not asserted equivalent. 0052 supplies the
topologically Noetherian parenthetical.

A rational representative gives a germ at each eta_i; equal representatives
have equal germs since every dense equality witness contains the eta_i.
For surjectivity, represent any prescribed germs on neighbourhoods W_i,
shrink to W_i intersect V_i, and glue on the disjoint dense union by the
outgoing-morphism mapping property of 01JB. For injectivity, equal germs
give equality neighbourhoods T_i inside the two domains; their union is a
dense equality witness. The structure-map equation for an S-morphism holds
on each covering piece and hence on the glued map. Thus arbitrary Y and
the relative version are covered; no finite-type, reducedness, or
separatedness hypotheses enter.

If X is empty, there are no components: its unique morphism to Y corresponds
to the empty tuple. The empty product of sets is a singleton.

### 7.1.8: Artinianness and the precise local factors

A Noetherian scheme has finitely many components by 0BA8. By 01RV,
R(X) = product_i B_i, where B_i = O_(X,eta_i). The affine arguments of 7.1.5
apply at each component generic point: the corresponding prime is minimal,
B_i is Noetherian by 01OW/00FN and has dimension zero by 00EU.
Thus 00KH makes each B_i Artinian local; 00KJ(4) makes the finite product
Artinian. Its maximal ideals are the products having the maximal ideal of
B_i in exactly one position. Localization at that ideal kills the other
idempotent factors and gives precisely B_i. This proves the local-components
clause as well as Artinianness. For empty X the ring is the zero ring, the
empty product of rings, which is Artinian.

### 7.1.9.1 and the parent-owned deduction of 7.1.9

For nonzero A with finitely many minimal primes p_i, put
Sigma = A minus their union. This is multiplicative. By 00ES and the
finite-component argument, D(f) is dense exactly when it contains every p_i,
equivalently f belongs to Sigma. If U is dense, write its complement as V(I).
Then I is contained in none of the p_i. Finite prime avoidance 00DS selects
f in I intersect Sigma, and dense D(f) lies inside U. This is the complete
lemma, with its prime-avoidance proof. Finitely many minimal primes suffice;
Noetherianity and reducedness are not needed for this derivation.

Now use the dense-open section colimit displayed in the proof of 01RV.
Cofinality of these principal opens, 01HV(3),(5) for their sections and
restrictions, and 00CR give
R(Spec(A)) = colim_(f in Sigma) A_f = Sigma^(-1)A.
The divisibility preorder is directed by products. Specializing 00CR from
modules to M=A respects ring operations, since all transitions are ring
homomorphisms. Directly, every rational function restricts to some A_f.
If a/f and b/g agree on a dense open witness, choose D(h) inside that
witness. In A_h their cross-difference vanishes, so some h^N kills ag-bf;
this proves equality in Sigma^(-1)A. Thus the identification is canonical.

This is the deduction at French 167–175, owned by 7.1.9 even though it
follows the statement of 7.1.9.1. For A=0, the spectrum is empty,
Sigma=A (the complement of the empty union), and its localization and
rational-function ring are both zero. The cofinality claim follows directly
using f=0; no prime avoidance with a hidden nonempty family is invoked.

## Hypotheses that must not be erased

- Irreducible does not mean integral: dual numbers k[e]/(e^2) give a
  one-point irreducible scheme whose rational ring is not a field.
- For A=k direct-sum V with V^2=0 and V infinite-dimensional, Spec(A) is
  topologically Noetherian but A=R(Spec(A)) is not Artinian; descending
  subspaces of V give descending ideals. Nilpotence alone is insufficient.
- For projective one-space, global regular functions form k while rational
  functions form k(t). The affine qualification in 7.1.5 is essential.
- An infinite disjoint union of field points is locally Noetherian, but its
  rational ring is an infinite product of fields and is not Artinian.
- The full 0BX8 local-ring-map comparison retains its locally finite type
  injectivity, locally finite presentation bijectivity, or locally finite
  type plus reduced-source bijectivity hypotheses. These do not apply to
  the different, unrestricted correspondence with morphism germs.
- For A=k[t,e]/(e^2,t*e), the unique minimal prime is (e), while t is a
  zerodivisor. The topologically dense D(t) is not schematically dense.
  Sigma^(-1)A=k(t), whereas e survives in the total quotient ring
  A_(t,e). Thus 02LX (union of minimal primes equals the zerodivisors)
  and 0EMF (no embedded weakly associated points, plus local finite-component
  control) are negative comparisons, not unconditional substitutes.
